WORKSHOP NOTES — LOCAL DEMO ONLY

These small stories were written for this project to test next-token training. They are invented workshop scenes, not records of experiments or a research corpus.

Note 1: Measure the shelf

On the first morning, Mira met Tavi at the blue bench. There was a wooden ruler beside a folded cloth. They wanted to measure the shelf, but first they cleared a space to work. A clear space made the small pieces easier to find.

"What do we know?" asked Mira. "We know what happened on this attempt," said Tavi. "We do not yet know whether it will happen every time." They made a short note, checked the result, and kept the earlier note instead of hiding the mistake. The first mark was too close to the edge, so we erased it and tried again.

The work took patience. Mira tried one change while Tavi watched. When they changed two things at once, they could not tell which one had helped. They returned to the simpler arrangement and repeated the check. The next note described the change and the result separately.

At the end they put the tools away, swept the floor, and left the notebook open to the next page. A finished task was useful, but an honest record was useful too. Tomorrow someone else could read the note and begin without guessing.
