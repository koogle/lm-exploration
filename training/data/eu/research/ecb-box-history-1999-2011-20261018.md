The pinned inventory has 967 qualifying candidates in this added range.
That is a discovery opportunity, not measured saved yield. The probe selection
excluded generic forecasts/technical-assumption titles and is not a random
sample from which to forecast a recovery rate.

The fallback now proposes recognized Monthly Bulletin filenames from 1999–2011
in addition to its prior range. Matching issue/year, valid month and box number,
PDF magic, at most 20 pages and full opening-title validation remain mandatory.
Earlier Economic Bulletin filename families remain excluded. The previously
observed 2011 long-title mismatch remains rejected by the unchanged title gate;
a regression test covers that case after admitting its year. No title is shortened
or rewritten to make a replacement pass.

Existing primary-404 alternate eligibility, cooldowns and attempts are preserved.
Completed candidates are not reset and no independent writer is introduced.
New documents remain subject to organization-wide exact text deduplication.
No website-exhaustion or passage-level novelty claim follows from these probes.

The expanded rule passed 279 tests plus Ruff lint/format and whitespace checks.
Deployment receipts confirmed the existing collection and manual apps and the
recovery app with its schedule disabled. This is deployment evidence, not a claim
that the 967 candidates have already been collected.
