Assessment reports and product information contribute 84% of stored words. This
supports prioritizing those series for the current token-growth objective while
keeping a coverage floor for the others. The 71 duplicate assessment candidates
matter: average unique assessment length is 49,947 words, but words per completed
candidate is 34,920. Neither should be extrapolated to all remaining documents
without accounting for changing content and duplication.

Language composition also matters. All 236 completed assessment candidates are
English. Product information spans 25 languages, with 8–10 completed documents
per language; overviews span 23 languages, with 9–11 per language. Higher
assessment weight therefore shifts the collection toward English. Higher product
information weight maintains multilingual output, though translated documents
remain related versions, not independent knowledge.

The latest completed round in the pinned state attempted 296 candidates in
3,004.39 seconds: 261 unique extractions, 26 duplicates, nine failures. This is
close to the ten-second pacing ceiling. Raising concurrent EMA requests would
not be the appropriate fix; choosing higher-yield candidates and avoiding known
unsupported formats improves output under the same source request budget.

## Failures and pending coverage

There are 46 failure records, representing 120 recorded attempts:

- 43 XLSX documents failed with `Start tag expected, '<' not found`. XLSX MIME
  strings contain `xml`, causing the generic parser to treat ZIP containers as
  XML. The adapter also previously mislabeled unknown suffixes as HTML.
- Two large assessment PDFs exceeded the 20 MiB cap: Hopledo and Onerji public
  assessment reports. Each was attempted three times.
- One Hungarian Cibinqo product-information URL reached the redirect limit after
  three attempts. A redirect loop was not independently verified.

## Scoped fix implemented

`adapters/ema.py` now filters unsupported document containers during new
enumeration and exposes `supported_record(record)` for saved manifests. Both
paths use the same format classifier, accept native text formats and PDF collision
suffixes, preserve news HTML, and reject XLS/XLSX/DOC/DOCX/ZIP or unknown attachment
formats before downloads. Existing document IDs and ordering remain unchanged.
Enumeration records unsupported-format counts and retains `complete=false` when
coverage gaps remain. No source records are marked completed by this filter.

The parent harness can apply this helper before scheduling saved candidates;
existing inventories need not be rewritten. Thirty adapter tests and scoped Ruff
checks pass. No XLSX flattening, request-size increase, HF mutations or Modal
launches were performed here. A separate bounded large-PDF extraction strategy
could recover the two oversized assessments later; it should not silently reset
all exhausted failures.
