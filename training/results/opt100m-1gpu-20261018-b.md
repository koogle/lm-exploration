just below the 100M ceiling to retain complete global batches. Same 124.4M model,

## Findings

Cropping alone improves throughput 12.3%. Cropped microbatch 2 improves it 69.6%
over baseline. Microbatch 4 is only 0.3% faster than 2 while increasing peak
allocation from 17.24 to 31.79 GiB; choose 2 for further validation.
Chunked loss reduces peak allocation by 26.5% versus cropped microbatch 2, but
is 7.3% slower. Keep it optional for memory-constrained runs. Cached RoPE adds
2.7% over cropped microbatch 2. Compilation adds 25.9% over cached RoPE and
produces the strongest measured combination. No recompile-limit, graph-break,
or fallback warning appeared in the retained continuation log. Small improvements
need repeated runs to distinguish them from run-to-run noise.

## Verification and limits

The CUDA correctness gate passed all ten FP32/BF16 cases: microbatches 1/2/4,
chunking, compilation, and cached RoPE against the reference loss and gradients.
Maximum absolute gradient error was 4.47e-8 for FP32 and 5.95e-4 for BF16, within
the declared tolerances. These tests use a small model and synthetic masking;
they do not prove bitwise equality of full training trajectories.

Every full variant restored a two-step checkpoint in a fresh process and matched
its saved diagnostic validation exactly. All variants consumed the same token
counts and per-source exposures. Final diagnostic losses ranged 3.53888–3.56974;
this is one fixed validation window per source, not evidence of quality parity.
The 24 local tests also passed, including exact next-update optimizer continuation.
Old production-source checkpoints still require the existing strict identity checks.

These are **single-GPU results**. Eight-rank loss/gradient checks, speedup,
communication costs and rank imbalance remain unverified. The queued eight-GPU
benchmark app was stopped, and the active 5B training application was untouched.
Recommend cropped microbatch 2 + cached RoPE + compilation for the next isolated
eight-GPU comparison; do not apply it to the active run based on these results.
