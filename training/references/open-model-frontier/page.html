How small can an agent be?

Nothing at or below 1B solves agentic tasks yet

Artificial Analysis ran Terminal-Bench Hard on every model it lists. All of them at 1.6B or below scored 0, including Gemma 3 270M, Granite 4.0 350M, Qwen3.5-0.8B, LFM2.5-1.2B, EXAONE 4.0 1.2B and MiniCPM5-1B. On Terminal-Bench 2.1 the best sub-1B results are noise: Qwen3.5-0.8B solved 1 of 267 trials (0.37) and K2 Horizon 0.9B 3 of 267 (1.12). No SWE-bench result exists for any model at or below 1B, not even a zero. Any reproducible solve at 720M–1B would be new.

The smallest working SWE agent is 1.7B

mini-coder-1.7b is Qwen3-1.7B fine-tuned on about 400k SWE-smith trajectories distilled from Qwen3-Coder-30B-A3B. It resolves 18.6% of SWE-bench Verified with a bash-only mini-SWE-agent, and 50.4% at pass@100. The untrained Qwen3-1.7B scores 0% under SWE-agent, OpenHands and mini-SWE-agent on the 50-task Verified Mini set. Our 720M agentic SFT run (356 trajectories) resolved 0 of 179 held-out PR tasks.

Trajectories, not parameters, move small agents

Below 10B, agentic fine-tuning outweighs size. Qwen2.5-Coder-7B goes from 1.0–1.8% on SWE-bench Verified to 52.7% as SWE-Hero-7B. Qwen3-4B-Instruct goes from 4.0% to 26.8% as mini-coder-4b and 38.9% as SWE-TRACE-4B (SFT plus RL). SWE-smith's 7B model reached 2.2% with 100 trajectories and about 17% with 2.4k.

Terminal tasks start to work at 2–4B

TMax-2B v1.1, trained with RL for the terminal, reports 9.4% on Terminal-Bench 2.0. Granite 4.2 3B scores 13.9% and K2 Horizon 3.7B 28.1% on 2.1, both measured by Artificial Analysis; K2 Horizon's own card says 39.7. Nanbeige4.2-3B claims 44.1% on 2.0 with no independent run. Read vendor and independent numbers separately; the gap between them is often 10 points.

Knowledge, math and code at our sizes

Narrow data sets the frontier below 1B

MobileLLM-R1-140M base, pretrained on mostly code and math, scores 15.9 on HumanEval and 16.3 on GSM8K. SmolLM2-135M, a general web model, scores 0.0 and 1.4. At 360M the gap holds: 32.9 / 39.4 against 0.0 / 3.2. Our thesis that a focused mix can reach the small-model frontier is consistent with this.

The targets at 150M and 720M

Best published base scores at ≤200M: HumanEval 15.9, MBPP 5.4, GSM8K 16.3, HellaSwag 42.1. At ≤750M (Falcon-H1-0.5B-Base): HumanEval 36.0, MBPP 52.1, GSM8K 60.2, MMLU 55.0. Fewer than four models report each sub-200M score, so that column is thin.

Knowledge tests sit at chance under 1B

The best GPQA Diamond under 750M is 30.6 against a 25% guess rate, and base MMLU-Pro tops out at 31 (10% chance). For 150M and 720M checkpoints, code pass@1, GSM8K, HellaSwag and held-out loss carry signal. MMLU-Pro, GPQA and SWE-bench do not.

Post-training moves small models furthest

Reasoning-tuned small models jump far above their base versions: Qwen3-0.6B reaches 77.6 on MATH-500 in thinking mode, and VibeThinker-1.5B reports AIME25 74.4 and LiveCodeBench 51.1. Above about 30B the frontier flattens. 27B dense models report LiveCodeBench and GPQA scores within a few points of trillion-parameter models.

Reading these numbers

  • Scores come from each model's card or tech report. A few come from another lab's comparison table; those entries say so in their notes (click a point).
  • Evaluation settings are not uniform. LiveCodeBench spans v3 to v6 and date windows. SWE-bench Verified scaffolds differ. HellaSwag and ARC-C mix 0-, 10- and 25-shot. Some cards report "GPQA" without saying Diamond.
  • Hybrid models appear twice, with thinking on (reasoning) and off (instruct). Base and post-trained checkpoints are separate points.
  • Total parameters include vision encoders for multimodal models and n-gram or Engram memory tables where a card counts them. Switch the x axis to active parameters to compare MoE compute per token.
  • Many cards from 2026 report agentic benchmarks only, so late frontier models often appear on GPQA Diamond alone. AIME 2026 scores are kept in notes, not plotted as AIME 2025.
  • Agentic scores depend on the harness, turn limit and number of attempts as much as on the model. Each point keeps its setting (hover or click). Where Artificial Analysis ran a model independently, that run is used over the vendor's number. Best-of-N, verifier-reranked, subset and expert-assisted results are left out.
  • Self-reported scores can be contaminated or tuned. Treat the frontier as an upper envelope of claims, not a controlled comparison.
